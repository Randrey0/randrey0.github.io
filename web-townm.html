Analysis and Development of a Web-Based Information System for Summarecon Serpong Town Management

This project involves the development of a web-based information system designed to address delays in information delivery and limited interaction between Summarecon Serpong Town Management and residents. Previously, information dissemination relied heavily on the company’s central website, limiting flexibility and responsiveness at the local management level.

In my role as a System/Program Analyst, I was responsible for analyzing existing business processes, identifying user requirements for both administrators and residents, and defining system solutions aligned with organizational needs. The analysis phase included observations, interviews, and requirement mapping to capture both functional and non-functional requirements. These findings were translated into system designs using ERD, UML diagrams (Use Case, Activity, and Class Diagrams), and Logical Record Structure (LRS).

The system was developed using the Prototyping Method, allowing continuous user feedback throughout iterative development cycles. Key features include news and announcement management, service request submission and tracking, user comments, galleries, advertising management, and an integrated chatbot for automated assistance.

The final system improves information delivery efficiency, streamlines service processes, and enhances communication between management and residents. This project demonstrates strong analytical skills, structured system design, and the ability to translate business requirements into effective and scalable technical solutions.
