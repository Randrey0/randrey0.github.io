Certificate BNSP Certificate Of Competency – Software Development (Program Analyst)

This Competency Certificate is officially issued by the Indonesian Professional Certification Authority (BNSP) through the Professional Certification Institution of Universitas Bina Sarana Informatika, certifying that I am competent in the field of Software Development with a specialization as a Program Analyst. The certification covers core competencies including system requirement analysis, scalable software analysis, algorithm implementation, SQL-based database access, debugging, program profiling, code review, and both unit and integration testing. This certificate is valid for three years and serves as formal recognition of professional skills aligned with national competency standards.
